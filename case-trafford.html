MEES benchmarking across 200 shopping centre units

Every unit in the UK's third largest indoor shopping centre brought to EPC compliance ahead of the deadline.

200EPCs delivered
100units brought up to standard
176,000sqm of retail space
Background

The Trafford Centre spans more than 176,000 sqm and 200 shop units, managed by international asset management company Pradera Lateral. Updated MEES regulations created significant exposure across the centre, with penalties for non-compliance reaching up to £150,000 per property.

Client: Pradera Lateral · The Trafford Centre  ·  Location: Manchester

What we did

  • An eight-step priority sequence, from valid EPCs on marketed units through to assessments on long-leasehold properties
  • Staged delivery structured around risk exposure and lease events
  • Close coordination with both the asset manager and the managing agent
Scope & approach

How the programme ran

Risk-prioritised EPC programme

Working with Pradera Lateral and managing agents Savills, we arranged access to each unit in risk priority order and undertook 200 EPCs.

Audit and risk assessment

Auditing all buildings let with F and G ratings, and risk-assessing those potentially rated F or G.

MEES study

Quantifying costs and exemptions ahead of the deadline, with recommended upgrades where appropriate.

Challenges

What made this difficult

01

Scale and access

200 individual units, each requiring arranged access and assessment within a fixed deadline.

02

Mixed compliance status

Units varied widely in rating and lease status, requiring a prioritised rather than uniform approach.

03

Hard deadline

Assessment and remediation had to complete before the February 2023 milestone.

Results

The outcome

100 units were brought up to standard as part of a staged delivery completed ahead of the February 2023 deadline, bringing every unit in the centre into EPC compliance with the updated MEES regulations.

What we learned

Lessons

Takeaways

  • Sequencing by risk and lease event beats working alphabetically through a portfolio
  • Coordination with managing agents is essential for access at scale
  • Complete visibility at every stage keeps a large programme on track
