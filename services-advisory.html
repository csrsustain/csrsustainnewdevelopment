Advisory & strategy

Turning survey findings into a costed, prioritised plan your board can approve — and your lenders and occupiers can be shown.

Decarbonisation planning

Knowing a building is non-compliant is not the same as knowing what to do about it. Advisory is where assessment becomes a capital plan — sequenced by risk, cost and return, and tied to the deadlines that actually apply to your assets.

Decarbonisation & net-zero roadmaps

An asset-by-asset route from where a building is today to where regulation and your own commitments require it to be — with interventions costed, sequenced and mapped against a realistic capital profile.

What's covered

  • Baseline energy and carbon profile
  • Intervention options with capex and payback
  • Phased delivery programme to target date
  • Alignment with corporate net-zero commitments

MEES compliance planning

With EPC B targeted for larger commercial buildings by 2031, the practical question is which assets to treat first. We rank the portfolio by letting risk and cost-to-comply, so capital goes where it protects the most value.

What's covered

  • Portfolio-wide MEES exposure ranking
  • Cost-to-comply modelling per asset
  • Stranding-risk and lettability analysis
  • Deadline-driven delivery sequencing

Energy audits & ESG reporting support

Detailed audits that find where energy is actually being lost, plus the evidence base to support ESG, SECR and investor reporting — measured performance rather than modelled assumption.

What's covered

  • Detailed site energy audits
  • Half-hourly consumption analysis
  • ESG and SECR reporting evidence
  • Occupier and lender disclosure packs

Capital planning & prioritisation

Most owners cannot do everything at once. We help decide what to do first — balancing compliance deadlines, plant condition, tenant disruption and return on capital.

What's covered

  • Multi-year capital programme
  • Risk-versus-return prioritisation
  • Plant-replacement lifecycle planning
  • Business cases for investment committee

Common questions

What owners usually ask

Q

How long does a roadmap take to produce?

[ Typically X–Y weeks for a single asset, longer for a portfolio. ] Timescales depend on data availability and how many buildings are in scope.

Q

Do you assume we'll use you for the works?

No. The roadmap is yours to deliver however you choose. We can design and manage delivery if you want us to — but the advice is written to be independent of that decision.

Q

What if the regulations change again?

They will. Roadmaps are built to be re-run rather than rewritten, and we flag where a change in the standard would alter the priority order.
