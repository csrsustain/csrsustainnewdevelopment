The owner's challenge

A decade of mandatory upgrade — and a fragmented supply chain

Owners face converging pressures on asset value. Meeting them piecemeal, through a dozen disconnected suppliers, is slow, costly and hard to evidence.

Regulatory deadlines

MEES tightening toward EPC B by 2031. Sub-standard assets risk becoming unlettable and stranded.

Rising energy cost

Ageing plant and inefficient HVAC quietly erode net operating income year after year.

ESG & investor scrutiny

Owners must evidence carbon performance to funds, lenders and occupiers — with real data.

Fragmented delivery

Surveyors, consultants, designers and contractors rarely join up. No single line of accountability.
