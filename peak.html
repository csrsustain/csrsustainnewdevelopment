CSR PEAK

We don't just certify your building — we keep it performing. Continuous monitoring and expert HVAC advisory, as an ongoing service.

Continuous optimisation

A building drifts. Controls get overridden, plant degrades, occupancy patterns change, and within eighteen months an efficient building is quietly wasting money again. PEAK exists to stop that — combining automated fault detection with engineers who act on what it finds.

Continuous monitoring & fault detection

Automated fault detection and diagnostics (AFDD) monitors plant continuously, surfacing faults and efficiency losses as they occur rather than at the next annual inspection — when they have already cost you a year of wasted energy.

What's covered

  • Continuous AFDD across monitored plant
  • Real-time fault and anomaly alerts
  • Equipment health scoring
  • Indoor environment and comfort tracking

Expert HVAC advisory

Software alone changes nothing. The difference is our engineers reviewing what the platform finds, deciding what genuinely matters, and telling you exactly what to fix — in priority order, with the value at stake.

What's covered

  • Engineer-reviewed fault triage
  • Prioritised action recommendations
  • Contractor briefing and verification
  • Regular performance review meetings

Evidenced reporting

Every intervention is measured, so the savings you report are ones you can defend — to your board, your investors, your lenders and your occupiers.

What's covered

  • Energy, carbon and cost reporting
  • Before-and-after intervention verification
  • ESG and investor-grade evidence
  • Portfolio-level performance dashboards

How it's delivered

PEAK is an annual service: platform licence, configuration and onboarding, named account management and the HVAC advisory layer that turns data into action. It runs for as long as you want the building managed.

What's included

  • Platform licence and configuration
  • Onboarding and point mapping
  • Named account management
  • Ongoing engineering advisory
What you receive

Deliverables

Live performance visibility

A continuously updated view of how each monitored building and system is actually performing.

Prioritised action list

A regularly reviewed list of what to fix next, ranked by value at stake and ease of resolution.

Verified savings report

Periodic reporting that evidences energy, carbon and cost savings against a measured baseline.
