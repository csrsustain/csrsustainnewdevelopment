Optimising the future of Liverpool’s innovation hub

Following a £2M redevelopment, AI-led analysis identified £33,233 of annual energy efficiencies and 48+ tonnes of avoidable CO₂.

Background

CENTRAL TECH is a cornerstone of Liverpool's innovation ecosystem. Following a £2M redevelopment by Sciontec in 2025, the site was positioned as a world-class hub for life sciences and digital technology. CSR Sustain was engaged to ensure the building's performance reflected its high-tech purpose.

Client: Sciontec · CENTRAL TECH  ·  Location: Liverpool

What we did

  • Continuous AI monitoring layered over the existing BMS
  • Targeted recalibration of chilled beams and terminal units
  • Site and data insights combined to improve system reliability
  • Shift from reactive call-outs to predictive maintenance
Scope & approach

How the programme ran

BMS harmonisation

Analysis identified misalignments in Building Management System logic, with HVAC components operating sub-optimally.

Continuous AI monitoring

AI-based observation highlighted anomalies and minor faults that manual inspection would have overlooked.

Precision calibration

Performance data identified chilled beams and terminal units operating outside optimal parameters, leading to targeted recalibration.

Challenges

What made this difficult

01

Energy overlap

Heating and cooling systems were identified as competing in certain zones.

02

Environmental consistency

Variations in internal temperature and airflow were affecting occupant comfort.

03

Carbon emissions

Baseline operations indicated room for alignment with Net Zero ambitions.

04

Reactive maintenance

Existing patterns were identified as inefficient compared with predictive, data-driven strategies.

Results

The outcome

348,516 kWh of optimisation potential and 48+ tonnes of annual CO₂ avoidance identified, alongside £33,233 in annual energy efficiencies from optimising boilers and chillers — plus over £20,000 per year eliminated in BMS engineer call-out fees. The findings established a performance blueprint for Sciontec's wider estate, including Liverpool Science Park.

Lessons

Takeaways

  • Post-redevelopment is exactly when performance drift is easiest to catch
  • Highly serviced life science assets reward continuous optimisation
  • A single building can set the performance blueprint for a wider estate
